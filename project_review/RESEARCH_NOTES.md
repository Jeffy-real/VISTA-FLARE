## 1. Research Gap Analysis: How Vista Flare Relates to Prior Work

State-of-the-art literature in talent analytics and automated career guidance demonstrates four primary paradigms:
1. **Unstructured Resume Matching (e.g., APJFNN, PJFNN, CareerBERT):** These frameworks process free-text paragraphs using heavy RNN, CNN, or Transformer (BERT) backends. While effective for verbose CVs, they incur massive computational overhead, exhibit high inference latency (>150 ms), suffer from cold-start issues for fresh graduates without job histories, and are prone to keyword spoofing.
2. **Graph-Based Interaction Matching (e.g., DPGNN, MVCTN):** These systems model historical hiring interactions over bipartite or heterogeneous information networks. They excel on commercial platforms with millions of historical applications, but fail completely in standalone career counseling settings where user interaction edges do not pre-exist.
3. **Sequence-Based Career Trajectory Models (e.g., CP-PJF, LSTM):** Recurrent models treat career progressions as temporal paths. However, when applied to unordered skill inventories (as demonstrated by Vista Flare's 1D CNN and LSTM experiments), inductive sequential biases artificially degrade classification accuracy.
4. **Vista Flare's Contribution & Niche:**
   - **Structured Profile Representation:** Maps unordered skill inventories into normalized, proficiency-weighted continuous numerical vectors ($373$ skills).
   - **High-Accuracy, Low-Latency Classification:** Demonstrates that a feed-forward Deep Artificial Neural Network (MLP) achieves superior accuracy ($68.27\%$ top-1, $85.73\%$ top-3) with sub-millisecond inference latency ($0.336$ ms/sample), outperforming recurrent ($47.52\%$) and convolutional ($17.73\%$) architectures on non-sequential tabular skill data.
   - **End-to-End System Integration:** Uniquely couples deep learning role scoring directly with dynamic skill-gap remediation, learning roadmap generation, and live external market job ingestion (Arbeitnow, Remotive).
